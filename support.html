Echo Support

Help with the Echo reflection app.

Contact support

Open a support request and include the app version, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not post reflection text, contact details, or other sensitive personal information.

Open a support request

Common steps

  • Processing is waiting: open Settings → Sync & Processing to see the exact status and retry when appropriate.
  • A subscription is not recognized: open the subscription offer and choose Restore Purchases.
  • Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, microphone, speech, notifications, or Face ID are unavailable: review Echo’s access in the iOS Settings app.
  • You want to stop AI processing: open Settings → Connections & Privacy and choose Stop under AI processing.
  • You want to remove your data: delete individual reflections from their detail screen, or use Settings → Delete account to remove the account and its Echo data.

Response and privacy

Support requests are reviewed on a reasonable-effort basis. The support tracker is public so other users can benefit from general fixes. Never include private reflection content or account secrets. For a data-deletion request, use the in-app Delete account control rather than posting personal information.
