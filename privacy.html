Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

Echo is a private reflection app developed and operated by Jonathan. This policy explains the information Echo uses to provide the app and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

  • Account information: the identifier and, when Apple provides it, name or email associated with Sign in with Apple.
  • Reflections and generated content: transcribed reflection text, Echo readings, memories, links you create, and Daily Echoes.
  • Connected context: only when you choose to connect it, Echo may use a bounded selection of Calendar events, Reminders, and matched Contacts details relevant to a reflection. Echo does not upload your complete libraries. Raw audio, contact phone numbers, email or postal addresses, photos, and raw device-local record identifiers are not sent for AI processing.

Other information used to operate Echo

  • Subscription and transaction status supplied by Apple. Echo does not receive your payment-card details.
  • An installation identifier and Apple push token when you enable notifications.
  • Operational records such as processing status, request identifiers, usage-limit counters, and error categories. Echo diagnostics do not contain reflection text, prompts, or generated model responses.

How information is used

Echo uses this information only to authenticate your account, synchronize your data, create requested AI readings and memories, prepare Daily Echoes, enforce service and subscription limits, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, and keep the service reliable. Echo does not sell personal information, show advertising, track you across other companies’ apps or websites, or use your content for marketing.

AI processing and your consent

Echo asks for affirmative permission before sending personal content for AI processing. If you allow it, Supabase securely routes the selected working content and Fireworks AI processes it to generate Echo features. Fireworks states that prompts and generations for open-model inference are not logged or stored unless the customer explicitly opts in, and that API inputs are not used to train models without opt-in. Fireworks may retain operational metadata needed to run the service. You can stop future AI processing at any time in Echo under Settings → Connections & Privacy. Withdrawing permission also turns off Daily Echo processing.

Service providers

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, subscriptions, and push-notification delivery.
  • Supabase: account authentication, encrypted cloud storage, server processing, and notification registration.
  • Fireworks AI: model inference for readings, memories, and Daily Echoes after you grant permission.

These providers process information on Echo’s behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. Information may be processed in the United States.

Retention and deletion

Account content is retained while your Echo account exists so it can synchronize across your signed-in devices. Daily Echo Calendar and Reminders briefings are limited to today and tomorrow, stop being used after 36 hours, and are deleted as they expire. Temporary processing records are kept only as needed to complete, retry, audit, or safely cancel work.

You can delete an individual reflection in the app. You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account; this removes your Echo account data from the active service and clears Echo data from that device. Some limited security, transaction, or backup records may persist when required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or disaster recovery and are removed under the applicable retention schedule.

Your choices

You can disconnect Calendar, Reminders, or Contacts from Echo without changing the iOS permission, withdraw AI-processing consent, disable notifications, sign out, delete individual reflections, or delete your account. You can separately review or revoke iOS permissions in the Settings app.

Security

Echo uses encrypted network connections, authenticated service requests, row-level database access controls, iOS data protection for local processing files, and optional Face ID app locking. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Echo is not directed to children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, Echo will update the effective date and provide notice where required. For privacy questions or requests, visit Echo Support. Do not include reflection text or other sensitive personal information in a public support request.
